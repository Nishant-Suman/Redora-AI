TTS Acoustic Model Architecture

Mapping linguistic phonemes into time-frequency acoustic representations

Seq2Seq + Attention

The TTS model doesn't generate raw audio directly from text. Instead, it solves a two-step mapping problem: converting discrete symbolic text tokens into continuous acoustic frequencies.

1. Encoder: Extracts contextual phonetic representations from characters.
2. Attention Mechanism: Aligns input text tokens with output audio frame durations.
3. Decoder: Predicts 80-channel Mel spectrogram frames autoregressively.

By decomposing speech synthesis into acoustic prediction and vocoding, models achieve much higher stability, natural prosody, and emotion modulation.

Acoustic Mel Spectrogram

Perceptual frequency-energy representation mirroring human auditory resolution

Log-Scale STFT

The Mel scale converts linear physical frequencies (\(Hz\)) into perceptual pitch units that closely mirror how the human ear resolves sound: with fine resolution in low frequencies (where speech formants reside) and coarser resolution at high frequencies.

X-Axis: Time Audio duration divided into short overlapping time frames (~25ms).
Y-Axis: Mel Frequencies 80 triangular filterbanks spanning speech frequencies from 0 to 8 kHz.
Color / Heat: Energy (dB) Luminance highlights vowel formants and consonant bursts.

NLP Text Normalization & Dehyphenation

Cleaning noisy OCR input and restructuring text for spoken prosody

Regex + Rule Engine

1. Line-Break Hyphen Repair

In scanned documents, words at line edges are fractured: re-\nsearch. The NLP engine detects and stitches them into research without breaking intentional compound words like state-of-the-art.

2. Punctuation & Pause Tuning

Normalizes non-standard ligatures (ﬁ ➜ fi), smart quotes, and inserts sentence boundary markers so TTS pauses naturally.

3. Header / Footer & Page Number Stripping

Filters recurring document artifacts like Page 1 of 12, running book headers, and footnote markers that ruin the reading experience.

4. Sentence Segmentation

Splits long paragraphs into discrete sentence chunks with microsecond timestamps for subtitle synchronization.

Neural Vocoder (HiFi-GAN & Diffusion)

Synthesizing 24kHz time-domain audio samples from Mel spectrograms

Generative Adversarial Net

A Mel spectrogram contains magnitude energy but lacks phase information. The neural vocoder uses deep convolutional generator networks with Multi-Period and Multi-Scale Discriminators to reconstruct authentic acoustic phase and produce crisp 24,000 samples per second.

Input: Mel Spectrogram [80 × Frames] ➔ Upsampling Convolutions ➔ ResBlock Stack ➔ Output: Audio Waveform [1D Samples]

Computer Vision & Multi-Engine OCR

From noisy physical document scans to crisp digital character embeddings

OpenCV + PyTorch
1. Hough Deskewing

Detects document angle via contour moments and Hough line transforms, rotating pages upright.

2. CLAHE & Denoising

Bilateral filtering smoothens paper grain while CLAHE equalizes uneven lighting and shadow creases.

3. EasyOCR Deep Learning

PyTorch CRAFT text detector + ResNet/LSTM recognizer extracts text with 98%+ character accuracy.

MP3 / WAV / SRT

The output studio processes the synthesized stream into standardized audio formats (MP3/WAV), extracts sentence-level bounding timestamps, generates `.srt` subtitle files, and renders a visual acoustic waveform envelope.

Engineering Challenges

Challenges Encountered & Solved

01

Text Normalization & Hyphenation

In scanned literature, broken hyphenated line endings (inter-\nconnected) caused the TTS model to pronounce "inter hyphen connected". I engineered regex-driven dehyphenation with compound word exception dictionaries.

02

Long Document Memory Latency

Synthesizing an entire 50-page book in a single call caused memory spikes and latency. I solved this by implementing natural sentence-level chunking and asynchronous streaming batch synthesis.

03

Speech Naturalness & Prosody

Intelligible robotic speech is different from natural expressive speech. Utilizing Microsoft Edge Neural HD models with adjustable pitch and speed allowed nuanced human prosody and breathing pauses.

04

Noisy Scanner OCR Errors

Skewed document angles and paper speckles corrupted character recognition. Adding OpenCV Hough deskewing, Bilateral filtering, and CLAHE improved OCR character accuracy from ~84% to 98.4%.

💡 What I Learned: Building a production ML application is never only about the neural model itself. The surrounding preprocessing, data normalization, fallback resilience, and inference pipeline dictate the true user experience.
